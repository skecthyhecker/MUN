Delegate Handbook

नवदूत परिषद 1.0 · 16 November 2026 · Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute

Working handbook · refined with the background guides

1. The Parishad

Navdoot Parishad is the Model United Nations of Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute, Subroto Park. Nav — the new. Doot — the messenger. You are an envoy, not a spectator.

This edition opens the United Nations General Assembly with a fifty-nation matrix. Eight further councils — NCS, IP, UNDP, UNHRC, UNCSW, UNODC, IAEA, WTO — take ranked preferences and will receive agendas in the sessions ahead.

2. Date, venue, fee

Date: 16 November 2026. Venue: Senior Wing, Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute, Subroto Park, New Delhi — 110010. Fee: ₹5, paid to +91 84476 82098. After payment you will see a confirmation that we have received your money.

3. Registration and the matrix

Rank three committees. If UNGA is among them, rank up to three countries.

Delegates from AFGJI, TAFS (The Air Force School) and AFBBS may select any open seat, including the first eight nations and Pakistan.

Individual delegates and other schools see those nine seats as Blocked. Every other country remains Open until it is Taken.

A short Hold is placed on a country while payment is verified, so two delegates cannot take the same seat. Confirmation follows a recorded ₹5.

4. Dress

Western formal or Indian formal. Dark suits, sarees, kurtas with a jacket, school blazers where appropriate. No sportswear, no slippers, no slogan tees. The chair may ask a delegate to leave the floor until dress is corrected.

5. Procedure in brief

Roll call

Present, or Present and voting. Late arrival is noted.

Moderated caucus

A speaker’s list under the chair. Yields as the chair allows.

Unmoderated caucus

Lobbying. Drafting. No shouting across the room. Return to your seat when the gavel falls.

Points and motions

Point of order, personal privilege, parliamentary enquiry. Motions to caucus, to suspend, to close debate. The chair’s ruling stands unless appealed as the rules allow.

Draft resolutions

Sponsors and signatories as the committee guide sets. Operative clauses do the work; preambulatory clauses set the ground.

6. Conduct

Diplomatic language. No personal attacks. No recording of other delegates without consent. Phones silent. The Secretariat may withdraw a confirmation, without refund, for serious misconduct. See the refund policy.

7. Awards

Best Delegate, High Commendation, Special Mention, and Verbal Mention may be awarded per council at the discretion of the Executive Board. Awards are not a right of registration.

8. International Press

IP does not vote. IP reports. Photographers wear the press lanyard and do not enter a huddle uninvited.

9. Crisis (NCS)

Directives, not long drafts. Updates from crisis staff are binding on the room. Keep your pad ready.

10. What to bring

School ID. A notepad. A pen. A bottle of water. The kit, badge, and meals are in the fee. Laptops are allowed at the chair’s discretion; they are not a right on the floor.
