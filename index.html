Before You Ask (FAQ)

What's included in the delegate fee? +
Your delegate kit, both days of committee sessions, meals across the conference, and entry to the closing ceremony. Accommodation is not included, but we can point outstation delegates to nearby stay options on request.
Can I register without a team? +
Yes, absolutely! Individual delegates are given complete allocation support and integrated seamlessly into committees.
What if I'm a first-timer with zero MUN experience? +
We run orientation workshops before executive board sessions to ensure first-time delegates feel confident in rules of procedure.
How does committee and portfolio allotment work? +
You select up to three preferences during registration. Allotment operates on a first-pay, first-served mechanism matched with your background.
What's the refund policy? +
Fees are strictly non-refundable due to early venue and logistics lock-ins. You may opt for delegate replacement 2–3 days prior.
Where exactly is the venue? +
Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute (AFGJI), Subroto Park, New Delhi - 110010.
