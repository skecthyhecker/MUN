The Vision

A Council Cast in the Colours of Bharat

NAVDOOT 1.0 is Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute's premier Model United Nations conference — a gathering where the discipline of diplomacy meets the depth of Indian tradition. Delegates step into the chappals of nations, negotiating crises, drafting resolutions, and finding, in argument, the art of listening.

In its first edition, NAVDOOT convenes its flagship committee — the United Nations General Assembly — with eight more councils awaiting their gavel in the sessions ahead.
