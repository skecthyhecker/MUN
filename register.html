The Verification Engine

Register for UNGA

Committee: United Nations General Assembly — the only council open for NAVDOOT 1.0.

How allocation works: Eight founding nations and Pakistan are reserved by the Secretariat and are not open for allocation. The remaining portfolios are split across four pools — Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute (host), The Air Force School, Air Force Bal Bharti School, and an Open pool for every other school and independent delegate. Your preferences are checked against your pool's quota, in the order you submit them.

Note on this demo: this page runs entirely in your browser as a live simulation of the verification engine described in the brief. It resets when the page reloads and is not shared between visitors — a production deployment would connect this same logic to a real database so allocations persist and stay in sync for every delegate and Secretariat member.
