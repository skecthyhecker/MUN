Refund Policy

AFGJI MUN · Navdoot Parishad 2026

Effective for AFGJI MUN 2026 · Last updated August 2026

As we gear up for the upcoming The Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute Model United Nations (NAVDOOT PARISHAD) 2026 conference, we would like to bring to your attention our refund policy and the process for delegate replacements.

Registration Fee Refund Policy

Upon registering as a delegate for AFGJI MUN 2026, please note that the registration fees are non-refundable. This policy is in place due to the nature of our event, where seat reservations and logistical arrangements are made promptly upon receiving your registration.

We allocate resources and plan meticulously to provide each delegate with a seamless and enriching experience. Hence, we regret that we are unable to offer refunds once the registration process is completed.

The fee is ₹5, payable to +91 84476 82098 by UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking. After the transaction is recorded you will see a confirmation that we have received your money. That confirmation is not a refundable receipt.

Delegate Replacement Process

We understand that circumstances can change, and you might find it necessary to have someone else attend the conference on your behalf. In such cases, we provide an alternative option that allows you to transfer your delegate spot to another individual. This individual should not be someone who has already registered for the conference.

To facilitate this process, we kindly request that you follow these steps:

  1. Contact our Delegate Affairs Team — To initiate the delegate replacement process, please get in touch with our Delegate Affairs Team at reetpupneja5@gmail.com.
  2. Explain Your Reason — In your communication, provide a clear and concise explanation for the need to transfer your spot.
  3. Provide Replacement Details — Please include comprehensive details about the person whom you wish to send as a replacement: full name, contact information, and any relevant background information.

We want to assure you that we will handle your request with the utmost professionalism and efficiency. You will get the delegate replacement done within 2–3 days of your email. Our aim is to accommodate these changes while maintaining the quality and integrity of our MUN for all participants.

Portfolio and committee changes

Once a UNGA country is confirmed in the matrix it is Taken. A change of portfolio is not a refund. Write to Delegate Affairs with your registration ID. The Secretariat may offer an alternative open seat; it is not obliged to restore a blocked or taken country.

Conduct

Registration implies acceptance of the handbook: dress, diplomacy, and the authority of the chair. The Secretariat may withdraw a confirmation, without refund, in cases of serious misconduct.

Contact Us

We sincerely appreciate your understanding of our refund policy and your cooperation in following the delegate replacement procedure outlined above. If you have any questions or require further assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out.
