Refund Policy & Delegate Replacement Procedure

Effective for AFGJI MUN 2026 · Last updated August 2026

As we gear up for the upcoming The Air Force Golden Jubilee Institute Model United Nations (NAVDOOT PARISHAD) 2026 conference, we would like to bring to your attention our refund policy and the process for delegate replacements.

Registration Fee Refund Policy

Upon registering as a delegate for AFGJI MUN 2026, please note that the registration fees are non-refundable. This policy is in place due to the nature of our event, where seat reservations and logistical arrangements are made promptly upon receiving your registration.

We allocate resources and plan meticulously to provide each delegate with a seamless and enriching experience. Hence, we regret that we are unable to offer refunds once the registration process is completed.

Delegate Replacement Process

We understand that circumstances can change, and you might find it necessary to have someone else attend the conference on your behalf. In such cases, we provide an alternative option that allows you to transfer your delegate spot to another individual. This individual should not be someone who has already registered for the conference.

To facilitate this process, we kindly request that you follow these steps:

  • Contact our Delegate Affairs Team: Email us at reetpupneja5@gmail.com
  • Explain Your Reason: Provide a clear and concise explanation for the need to transfer your spot.
  • Provide Replacement Details: Include full name, contact information, and background details of the replacement delegate.

You will get the delegate replacement done within 2–3 days of your email.
